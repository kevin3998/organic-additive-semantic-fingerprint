# Figure legend draft — v2

**Fig. X | Semantic reranking of organic electrolyte additive candidates.**
**a,** Upper, Stage 1 anchor-prescreen rank versus deterministic final semantic
rank for all 200 retained candidates. The dashed diagonal denotes unchanged
rank, and the pale band denotes the final Top 20. The selected candidate moves
from Stage 1 rank 141 to final semantic rank 1; the descriptive Spearman
correlation between the two rankings is 0.014. Lower, final-rank-ordered
semantic fingerprint landscape showing the NCM811-interface, lithium-metal-
interface and LHCE-solvation percentiles and their equal-weight mean for the
same 200 candidates. Dashed lines mark ranks 20 and 50. **b,** Axis percentiles
for the final Top 20 candidates, identified in the plotting area by their
deterministic final ranks. Cell text gives the corresponding within-axis rank.
The rank-1 row is emphasized; the complete rank-to-identity mapping is provided
in Source Data. **c,** Cosine similarity of the rank-1
candidate to the ten reference additives under each electrolyte criterion.
Outlined cells are the two highest similarities used to calculate each
axis-specific score. **d,** Pairwise Spearman rank correlations among the
NCM811-interface, lithium-metal-interface and LHCE-solvation percentiles for all
200 candidates. Correlations are descriptive; no inferential test was applied
because the data represent one deterministic locked screening run. Exact
semantic-score ties are resolved by anonymous candidate identifier and do not
introduce an additional chemical score. Source data are provided as a Source
Data file.
