# Figure legend draft — v1

**Fig. X | Semantic-fingerprint ranking of organic electrolyte additive
candidates.** **a,** Deterministic ranking of all 200 candidates retained after
Stage 1. Coloured points show the NCM811-interface, lithium-metal-interface and
LHCE-solvation percentiles; the black line is their equal-weight mean. The
rank-1 candidate is annotated after ranking. **b,** Axis percentiles for the
final Top 20 candidates. Cell text gives the corresponding within-axis rank,
and the rank-1 row is emphasized. **c,** Cosine similarity of the rank-1
candidate to the ten reference additives under each electrolyte criterion.
Outlined cells are the two highest similarities used to calculate the
axis-specific score. **d,** Relationship between structural and semantic scores
for all 200 candidates. Open violet symbols identify the 52 candidates in 24
exact semantic-tie groups; structural similarity was used only to order
candidates within these groups. The rank-1 candidate has no semantic tie and
therefore does not use the structural tie-breaker. Spearman's correlation is
descriptive; no inferential test was applied because the data represent one
deterministic locked screening run. Source data are provided as a Source Data
file.
