# Figure legend draft — v3

**Fig. X | Three-axis semantic screening identifies a consistently leading
electrolyte-additive candidate.** **a,** Stage 1 anchor-prescreen rank versus
deterministic final semantic rank for all 200 retained candidates. The dashed
diagonal denotes unchanged rank and the pale band denotes the final Top 20.
After anonymous ranking, the leading candidate was identified as
3-cyanotetrahydrofuran and moved from Stage 1 rank 141 to semantic rank 1.
Stage 1 is used only to retain the Top 200; its score does not enter the final
semantic score.
**b,** Raw Top-2 reference-anchor cosine scores under the NCM811-interface,
lithium-metal-interface and LHCE-solvation criteria (n = 200 candidates per
axis). Pale blue-violet points denote individual candidates, coloured
horizontal segments denote the interquartile range and black ticks denote the
median. Filled coral and open black circles denote ranks 1 and 2, respectively;
Δ reports their descriptive raw-score difference. Each axis uses its own
raw-score range. **c,** Three-axis percentiles for the deterministic Top 20.
Rows are labelled by final rank and
anonymous candidate ID; cell text gives within-axis rank. **d,** Post-ranking
cosine similarity of the rank-1 candidate to all ten reference additives under
each criterion (upper), and the number of candidates for which each anchor
entered the corresponding Top-2 pair (lower; 400 selections per axis). Coral
outlines identify the two similarities
used for the rank-1 candidate's axis score. The three criteria are
context-conditioned views and are strongly correlated (Spearman
ρ = 0.912–0.950); they are not assumed to be statistically independent. No
inferential test was applied to this deterministic model run. Exact
semantic-score ties are ordered by anonymous candidate identifier; no
structural-similarity score enters the ranking. Source data are provided as a
Source Data file.
