# Figure legend draft — v4

**Fig. X | Three-axis semantic screening identifies a consistently leading
electrolyte-additive candidate.** **a,** Stage 1 anchor-prescreen rank versus
deterministic final semantic rank for all 200 retained candidates. The dashed
diagonal denotes unchanged rank and the pale band denotes final ranks 1–20.
The orange marker denotes the candidate subsequently selected at semantic rank
1; it moved from Stage 1 rank 141. Stage 1 is used only to retain the Top 200
and does not enter the final semantic score. **b,** Raw Top-2 reference-anchor
cosine scores under the NCM811-interface, lithium-metal-interface and
LHCE-solvation criteria (n = 200 candidates per axis). Points denote individual
candidates, coloured horizontal segments denote the interquartile range and
black ticks denote the median. Filled orange and open black circles denote ranks
1 and 2, respectively; Δ reports their descriptive raw-score difference. Each
axis uses the same raw cosine-score range. **c,** Rotated parallel-coordinate view of
all 200 candidates. Each trajectory connects the NCM811, lithium-metal and LHCE
within-axis percentiles to their equal-weight mean semantic score. The dark-coral
trajectory, drawn at the same width as all other trajectories, denotes the candidate
at rank 1 on every axis and after aggregation.
**d,** Post-ranking cosine similarity of the rank-1 candidate to all ten
reference additives under each criterion (upper), and the number of the 200
Stage-2 candidates for which each anchor entered the corresponding Top-2 pair
(lower; 400 selections per axis). Orange outlines identify the two similarities
used for each rank-1 axis score. The three criteria are context-conditioned
views and are strongly correlated; they are not assumed to be statistically
independent. No inferential test was applied to this deterministic model run.
Exact semantic-score ties are ordered by anonymous candidate identifier; no
structural-similarity score enters the ranking. Source data are provided as a
Source Data file.
